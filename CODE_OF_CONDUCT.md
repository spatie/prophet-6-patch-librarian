# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Treat others with respect, give and accept constructive feedback, and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions and warn or ban participants who violate these standards.

To report a violation, email [info@spatie.be](mailto:info@spatie.be) rather than filing a public issue. Maintainers will respect the privacy of anyone making a report.
